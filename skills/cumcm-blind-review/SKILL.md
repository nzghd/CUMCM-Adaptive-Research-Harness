# CUMCM Blind Independent Review

Export the final scientific artifacts without prior rankings, reviewer opinions or Agent/provider history. Review them in a fresh session or by a different human/model.

The blind reviewer must attack:

- problem interpretation and omitted structural alternatives;
- assumptions, derivations, dimensions and boundary cases;
- proof provenance for bounds, optimality, convergence, significance and robustness;
- model/code consistency and reproducibility;
- simple baselines and counterexamples;
- conclusions beyond tested scope.

An internal same-session self-review may still be useful, but it is not a blind independent review.
