# Robust and Multiobjective Optimization

**Use when:** decisions trade off multiple goals or must remain useful under
uncertainty.

**Formulation:** define uncertainty set/distribution, objective vector, dominance,
scalarization or risk measure, and whether robustness is worst-case, chance-
constrained or distributional.

**Baseline:** nominal single-objective solution and simple weighted sum.

**Required diagnostics:** Pareto front stability, normalization, weight/scheme
sensitivity, price of robustness, out-of-sample scenarios and feasibility under
uncertainty.

**Common traps:** arbitrary weights presented as objective truth; one Pareto point;
robustness claimed from a few random scenarios; incomparable objective scales.
