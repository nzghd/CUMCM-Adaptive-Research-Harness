# Graph / Network Modeling

**Use when:** entities and relationships, routes, flows, diffusion or connectivity
are central.

**Formulation:** define nodes, edges, direction, weights, capacities, time variation
and graph-derived decision variables.

**Baseline:** shortest path, spanning tree, degree/centrality or flow baseline as
appropriate.

**Required diagnostics:** graph construction sensitivity, disconnected cases,
capacity/flow conservation, complexity, small exact instances and robustness to
edge perturbation.

**Common traps:** arbitrary edges/thresholds; centrality interpreted causally;
undirected treatment of directed processes; ignoring disconnected components.
