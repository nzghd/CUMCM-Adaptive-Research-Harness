# Statistical Inference

**Use when:** estimation, uncertainty, group comparison or relationship inference
is the main task.

**Formulation:** define population, sampling unit, response, predictors, estimand,
likelihood/loss and assumptions.

**Baseline:** descriptive statistics and a simple interpretable model.

**Required diagnostics:** leakage-safe split, residuals, calibration, uncertainty
intervals, multicollinearity, missingness mechanism, robustness to influential
points and multiplicity where relevant.

**Common traps:** causal language from correlation; p-values without effect size;
data leakage; repeated measures treated as independent; significance claimed
without a valid test.
